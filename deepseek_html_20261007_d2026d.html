Prototype Note: FirePath is an academic prototype developed to demonstrate zone-based fire detection, evacuation assessment and IoT monitoring. The HC-SR04 exit measurement is used as a prototype representation of exit availability. MQ-2 and flame sensors are low-cost development sensors and are not substitutes for certified fire detection systems. The system should not be treated as a replacement for legally required fire safety infrastructure.
“FirePath changes fire detection from a simple alarm system into a situation-aware evacuation assistance system.”
